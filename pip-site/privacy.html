Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This policy explains what pip ("pip", "we", "us") collects when you use the pip bot on Discord or Telegram and this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using pip you agree to this policy.

The short version. pip stores the questions you ask it and the answers it gives, scoped to the server or DM they came from, so it can remember context and improve. It never asks for or stores wallet keys, seed phrases, or private funds. It never places trades. We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

pip is an AI market-analysis bot operated by its creator (the "operator"). For any privacy question, contact us via the channels in section 10.

2. What we collect

  • Messages to pip and its replies. When you @mention pip, DM it, or reply to it, we store your question text and pip's answer, together with the platform (Discord/Telegram), the server/chat identifier, and your platform user identifier. This is what powers memory and context.
  • Usage metadata. Timestamps, which tools ran, response latency, token/cost accounting, and success/failure — used to operate, debug, and rate-limit the service.
  • Reactions. If you 👍/👎 a pip answer, we record that as feedback to improve quality.
  • Account & billing data. If you subscribe, the platform handle you enter at checkout and a payment reference from our payment providers. Card and crypto payment details are handled entirely by those providers — we never see or store full card numbers or wallet private keys.

We do not collect the contents of channels pip is not mentioned in, your wallet keys or seed phrases, or messages that never involve pip.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and provide grounded market analysis.
  • To maintain per-server and per-user memory so answers get more relevant over time.
  • To operate the service: rate limits, abuse prevention, cost control, and debugging.
  • To provision and manage paid access after payment.
  • To understand aggregate, anonymized usage trends (e.g. which topics are popular) — without identifying individuals.

4. Memory scope

Memory is siloed. A server's memory is used only to answer within that server; a person's history in a chat is used only to serve that same person. One community never sees another's data, and one user's questions are never surfaced to a different user.

5. AI processing

To generate answers, your question and relevant context are sent to our AI model provider (Anthropic) and, where a question requires it, to third-party market-data and web-search providers. These providers process the request to return a result and are not permitted to use it to train their models on your data under our agreements. A list of data sources is described in our docs.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments (card payments, as merchant of record) and Loop Crypto (USDC payments). When you check out, you are subject to their terms and privacy policies. They share back only what we need to unlock your access (a payment/subscription status and the handle you provided). We never receive your full card details or crypto private keys.

7. Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with: the infrastructure and AI providers needed to run pip (hosting, database, model, market-data and payment providers, acting on our instructions), or where required by law. Discord and Telegram themselves are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Retention

We keep conversation and usage records for as long as needed to provide memory and operate the service. You can ask us to delete your data (section 9); we will remove your stored questions, answers, and profile associated with your platform identifier, except records we must keep for legal, security, or billing reasons.

9. Your choices & rights

  • Access / deletion. Ask us to export or delete the data associated with your account.
  • Stop using pip. Remove the bot from your server, or stop messaging it, at any time.
  • Cancel billing. Manage or cancel a subscription through the link in your receipt.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g. under GDPR or similar laws). We honor valid requests.

10. Contact

Questions, or a data request? Reach us on Discord or Telegram. (A support email can be added here.)

11. Changes

We may update this policy as pip evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
