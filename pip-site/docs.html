Get started with pip.

pip is an AI market analyst that lives in your Discord or Telegram. Add it, @mention it, and ask. This page covers setup, how to talk to it, what it can answer, plans, and privacy.

1 · Add pip

pip runs on two surfaces. Add it wherever your community lives, the same analyst, the same memory model on each.

Discord

Invite the bot to your server (you need Manage Server). Once it's in, @mention it in any channel it can see, or DM it directly.

How to DM pip: Discord only lets you DM a bot you share a server with: you can't message it out of nowhere. So first either add pip to a server you manage (above), or join the community Discord, where pip already lives, that's enough to share a server with it. Then open pip's profile and hit Message, and just ask your question, no @mention needed in a DM.

Telegram

Open @pipinvestbot and send /start. In groups, add the bot and @mention it (or reply to its messages).

2 · How to ask

There's no command syntax to learn, you talk to pip in plain language.

  • In a server: @pip what's funding on BTC perps right now?
  • In a DM: just send the question, no @mention needed.
  • Follow-ups: reply to one of pip's messages and it keeps the context, "render that on a chart", "what about ETH?"
  • Charts & screenshots: attach a chart image and ask pip to read it, or ask it to draw one.
  • Contract addresses: paste a token address and pip runs a safety + liquidity scan.

3 · What it can do

One analyst for the whole tape. If it trades, or moves what trades, pip covers it, always grounded in live data pulled the moment you ask, never made up.

  • Prices & structure: spot & perp prices, levels, RSI/EMA/MACD/VWAP reads on any timeframe.
  • Derivatives: live funding & open interest (including Hyperliquid builder markets), the positioning lens.
  • Macro & events: next FOMC / CPI dates, rate-decision context, key macro indicators.
  • Equities: quotes, fundamentals, earnings dates & surprises for listed names that move crypto.
  • Token scans: liquidity, holders, concentration, honeypot / authority checks, smart-money flow on a pasted contract.
  • Charts: branded candlestick charts with indicators, drawn on request.
  • Event & prediction markets: market-implied odds for elections, sports and macro from Polymarket & Kalshi.
  • Live web: checks current facts and breaking headlines when the answer needs it.

pip is an analyst, not a guru, it won't hand you buy/sell signals or "setups." It gives you two-sided, grounded reads and stress-tests your own thesis.

4 · Memory

pip remembers per server and per person. Your community's watchlists, recurring tickers, and questions become memory that makes answers sharper over time, and it adapts to how you like to be answered. Memory is scoped: one server never sees another's, and one trader's history is never served to someone else.

5 · Plans & limits

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, no card required. Free tiers stay free with a daily question limit; paid plans lift the ceiling and unlock deeper reasoning, memory, chart reads and scans. See the current lineup on the pricing page.

  • Personal (DM): Free, Lite, Pro, pip one-on-one.
  • Server: Free, Pro, Elite, pip for the whole community.

After you subscribe, access unlocks automatically on the Discord or Telegram handle you enter at checkout, usually within a minute of payment.

6 · Privacy

pip only stores the questions asked to it and its own answers, scoped to the server or DM they came from, never your wallet, never your keys, never private channels it isn't in. Full detail in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

7 · FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. pip is an analytical tool. Everything it says is information, not advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. You are responsible for your own decisions.

Where does the data come from?

Live market, derivatives, macro and on-chain sources, pulled at question time and timestamped. pip synthesizes them into one read.

Can pip trade for me?

No. pip never touches funds, never places orders, and never asks for wallet keys or seed phrases. It reads and analyzes only.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel your subscription any time through the payment link in your receipt. Your trial never auto-charges without you confirming.

I paid but didn't get access.

Double-check the handle you entered at checkout matches your Discord username / Telegram handle exactly. Still stuck? Reach us on Discord.

pip provides market information and analysis only. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Markets are risky; you trade at your own risk.
